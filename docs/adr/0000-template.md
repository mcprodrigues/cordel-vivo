# ADR NNNN: Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Issue:** #N

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints from AGENTS.md and `docs/plano.md`, and what is known or still unmeasured. Numbers only if they were measured; otherwise write "to be measured".

## Decision

What we will do, stated in one or two sentences, followed by the details that matter.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to do because of this decision. Include the conditions under which we would revisit it.

## Alternatives

Each option that was seriously considered, and why it was not chosen.
